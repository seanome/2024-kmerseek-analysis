Evidence cards

SYNTHETIC FIXTURES, not data. Evaluation queries; cards kept by the frozen settings.

How to read a card: the match line marks '|' where query and target have the same amino acid, '.' where they differ and a blank at a gap. The highlighted letters are the measured stretch's residues that the match covers. The match says the two stretches share sequence; it does not say they have the same biological effect. Tile ends (Sanborn 2021) come from the library design, not from the residues the activation needs.
