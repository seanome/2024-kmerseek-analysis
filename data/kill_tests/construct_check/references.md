## No citation

`GS linker GSG` and `GS linker GGSGGS` have an empty `source_id`. Short glycine-serine linkers of these lengths are used widely and no single paper introduced them, so the table records them as synthetic with no citation rather than attaching a paper that does not describe that exact sequence.
