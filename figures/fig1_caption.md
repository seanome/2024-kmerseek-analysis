**Figure 1 | Between related proteins at 20–30% identity, the hydrophobic/polar (H/P) class of aligned residues is kept 2.3 times as far above chance as the amino acids themselves.**
Pairs of sequences from the same Pfam-A 38.2 seed alignment, with at least 50 aligned positions. H/P is kmerseek's hp_thomas_dill2 alphabet in every panel: hydrophobic ACFILMVWY, polar DEGHKNPQRST. Amber and blue squares are the H and P classes; purple marks H/P and grey the 20 amino acids.
**a**, One pair, RPIR_ECOLI residues 14–90 and Y143_HAEIN residues 5–81 (Pfam HTH_6, PF01418). 16 of 77 aligned residues are identical (grey ticks) and 56 of 77 have the same class (purple ticks). Its longest same-class run (aligned positions in a row, no gap, same class in both; black box) is 12 residues, the median of all 37,085 pairs at 20–30% identity, which is why it was chosen.
**b**, Cohen's kappa, the agreement of the classes of aligned residues corrected for chance (0 is the agreement expected from the two sequences' class shares; 1 is every aligned residue in the same class), mean over pairs, by percent identity (each bin includes its upper edge). At 20–30% identity (37,085 pairs from 15,276 families), kappa is 0.46 for H/P and 0.20 for the 20 amino acids; 95% bootstrap intervals over pairs are 0.4616–0.4635 and 0.1985–0.1992, shorter than the dots. H/P kappa is above amino-acid kappa in every bin, 3.4 times as high at ≤20% and 1.2 times at >60% identity.
**c**, What kmerseek seeds from, on one pair: LPOA_HAEIN (575 aa) and LPOA_VIBCH (603 aa), Pfam LppC (PF04348), 25% identity over the Pfam alignment. Dot plots of the full Swiss-Prot sequences: each dot is a word both proteins contain, placed at its start in each. Left, amino-acid 5-mers (2 shared); right, H/P 19-mers (7 shared). The two word lengths carry about the same information in Swiss-Prot 2026_03 (5 × 4.06 and 19 × 0.97 bits). The black box surrounds a 20-residue same-class run in which 2 residues are identical; no shared amino-acid 5-mer lies on its diagonal (0). The 7 H/P 19-mers overlap on that diagonal and join into one region (purple bar under LPOA_HAEIN): LPOA_HAEIN 280–304 with LPOA_VIBCH 287–311, all 25 positions in the same class, inside the Pfam domain (39–568). It is 5 residues longer than the run because the Pfam alignment puts a gap in LPOA_VIBCH next to it, where the ungapped diagonal pairs more same-class residues. The pair was chosen by a rule: of 20 pairs with a run of at least 19 that is not low-complexity and at most 30% identical, the one with the fewest shared H/P 19-mers off that diagonal.
