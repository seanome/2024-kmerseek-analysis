**Figure 1, option 2 | kmerseek places domains under 30 residues about as well as sequence aligners and three times as well as Foldseek, and falls behind every tool as domains get longer.**
**a**, as in option 1.
**b–d**, QfO Pfam-region benchmark, midi run: 964 human chromosome 6 proteins searched against 9 proteomes (mouse, chicken, zebrafish, *Ciona*, fly, worm, yeast, *Arabidopsis*, *E. coli*). A call is right when it carries the true Pfam-A family and overlaps the true domain with an intersection over union of at least 0.5; a domain counts only in a proteome that has its family. Fmax is the best F1 over each tool's score thresholds. Lines: mean over the 9 proteomes; vertical lines: range over the proteomes. kmerseek is hp_lehninger2 with the low-complexity mask, k = 29, the k with the highest Fmax over all domains on this benchmark, chosen after the fact; thin purple lines are k = 18–29. **b**, By domain length, drawn at the median length of each bin (16–30, 31–60, 61–120, 121–250, ≥251 aa). At 16–30 aa (1,902 domain instances over the 9 proteomes), Fmax is 0.054 for kmerseek, 0.056 phmmer, 0.050 MMseqs2, 0.074 HHblits and 0.018 Foldseek; at ≥251 aa it is 0.041 for kmerseek and 0.375–0.407 for the others. **c**, By the share of the protein's residues with AlphaFold pLDDT below 50, the pipeline's disorder measure (bins 0–0.1, 0.1–0.3, 0.3–0.6, 0.6–1, drawn at their middles): Foldseek falls from 0.31 to 0.15, phmmer from 0.35 to 0.25, kmerseek stays at 0.09–0.12. **d**, By the mean AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT) over the whole protein, in the pipeline's bins 50–70, 70–90 and 90–100, drawn at their middles; the bin below 50 holds 27 proteins, fewer than the 30 the pipeline needs to score a bin. Every other tool rises with pLDDT; kmerseek stays at 0.09–0.12. Disorder and pLDDT bins also differ in domain length (median 105 aa in the least disordered bin, 80 aa in the most; 75 aa at pLDDT 50–70, 122 aa at 90–100), so part of c and d is the length effect of b.
