**Figure 1, option 1 | At the same number of chance hits, exact amino-acid seeds reach more Pfam pairs at 20–30% identity than exact H/P seeds.**
**a**, `kmerseek pair` on CED-9 (P41958) against BCL2 (P10415), hp_lehninger2, k = 17: 3 shared 17-mers on one diagonal join into one region, CED-9 163–181 with BCL2 139–157, 5 of 19 residues identical and 19 of 19 in the same class (h, hydrophobic AFGILMPVWY; p, polar CDEHKNQRST). Purple bars: the region on each protein; white boxes: UniProt BH1 motifs (2026_03).
**b**, Each point is one k for one alphabet. x: human proteins (GENCODE v49 canonical, 19,732) that share at least one exact k-mer with a shuffled human protein (same length and composition), mean over 30 random queries; every such protein is a chance hit. y: share of the 37,085 Pfam-A 38.2 seed pairs at 20–30% identity whose alignment holds a run of k aligned positions in the same letter of the alphabet, which is a shared k-mer on the pair's own diagonal. Both axes are spaced by ratio. Filled markers: k values kmerseek tests for that alphabet (PR 101). Points with no chance hit or no pair reached are not drawn. At about 1 chance protein per query (dashed line; the labelled k is the smallest with a mean of at most 1), the share reached is protein20 1.0% (k = 9), dayhoff6 0.42% (k = 18), hp_lehninger2 0.18% (k = 32), hp_thomas_dill2 0.059% (k = 39). This draft uses a 30-query pilot; the 300-query run is on Sherlock.
**c**, At that k for each alphabet, the share of Pfam pairs sharing a seed by identity (each bin includes its upper edge). At 30% identity or less no alphabet reaches more than 1.0% of pairs; above 60% identity protein20 reaches 87.9% and hp_lehninger2 47.0%. The Pfam seed alignments are built by profile HMMs from amino-acid similarity, which may favour amino-acid runs over H/P runs.
