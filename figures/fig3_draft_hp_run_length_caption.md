**Figure 3 (draft panel) | At 20–30% identity, same-class runs are longer than independent positions would give, but few reach the length an H/P seed needs.**
The 37,085 Pfam-A 38.2 seed pairs at 20–30% identity, hp_lehninger2 (hydrophobic AFGILMPVWY, polar CDEHKNQRST). Top, share of pairs whose longest same-class run (aligned positions in a row, no gap, same class in both) is at least k; the vertical axis is spaced by ratio, so the step from 0.1% to 1% takes as much room as the step from 1% to 10%. Real pairs (solid line): mean longest run 12.0 residues (median 11); 7.6% of pairs reach k = 19 and 0.3% reach k = 30. Predicted from independent positions (open circles): for each pair, Pr(agree) is its share of aligned positions in the same class (mean 0.70); the curve is the mean over pairs of the exact probability of a run of at least k if each aligned position kept its class on its own with that pair's Pr(agree), runs stopping at alignment gaps. One sequence shuffled (dash-dot; 20 shuffles per pair, composition and gaps kept): mean longest run 6.3, 0.027% of pairs reach k = 19. Bottom, real divided by predicted: 1.54–1.82 at k = 17–35 (0.3% against 0.17% at k = 30). If each pair's aligned positions are instead joined into one unbroken stretch, the prediction is higher than the real pairs (real / predicted 0.71–1.00). Dashed line: k* = 28, the H/P k-mer length at which one chance match is expected across Swiss-Prot 2026_03; k* = ⌈log2 N / B_α⌉, with N = 209,008,777 residues and B_α = −log2(h_query·h_target + p_query·p_target) = 0.995 bits per letter, where h_query = h_target = 0.528 and p_query = p_target = 0.472 are the Swiss-Prot hydrophobic and polar shares. 0.53% of real pairs reach k*. Dotted line: mean longest run of real pairs.
