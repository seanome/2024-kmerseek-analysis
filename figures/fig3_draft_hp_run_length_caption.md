**Figure 3 (draft panel) | At 20–30% identity, same-class runs are about as long as independent positions would give, and rarely reach the length an H/P seed needs.**
The 37,085 Pfam-A 38.2 seed pairs at 20–30% identity, hp_thomas_dill2. Top, share of pairs whose longest same-class run (aligned positions in a row, no gap, same class in both) is at least k; the vertical axis is spaced by ratio, so the step from 0.1% to 1% takes as much room as the step from 1% to 10%. Real pairs (solid line): mean longest run 13.0 residues (median 12); 11.2% of pairs reach k = 19 and 0.63% reach k = 30. Predicted from independent positions (open circles): for each pair, Pr(agree) is its share of aligned positions in the same class (mean 0.74); the curve is the mean over pairs of the exact probability of a run of at least k if each aligned position kept its class on its own with that pair's Pr(agree), runs stopping at alignment gaps. One sequence shuffled (dash-dot; 20 shuffles per pair, composition and gaps kept): mean longest run 6.3, 0.013% of pairs reach k = 19. Bottom, real divided by predicted: 1.29–1.44 at k = 19–35 (0.63% against 0.44% at k = 30), so same-class positions cluster only a little. If each pair's aligned positions are instead joined into one unbroken stretch, the prediction is higher than the real pairs (real / predicted 0.50–0.78). Dashed line: k* = 29, the H/P k-mer length at which one chance match is expected across Swiss-Prot 2026_03; k* = ⌈log2 N / B_α⌉, with N = 209,008,777 residues and B_α = −log2(h_query·h_target + p_query·p_target) = 0.967 bits per letter, where h_query = h_target = 0.424 and p_query = p_target = 0.576 are the Swiss-Prot hydrophobic and polar shares. 0.82% of real pairs reach k*. Dotted line: mean longest run of real pairs.
