**Supplementary Figure | The previous Figure 1's examples, in hp_thomas_dill2.**
H/P is kmerseek's hp_thomas_dill2 alphabet in both panels: hydrophobic ACFILMVWY, polar DEGHKNPQRST. Amber and blue squares are the H and P classes; purple marks H/P and grey the 20 amino acids. Pairs of sequences from the same Pfam-A 38.2 seed alignment.
**a**, One pair, RPIR_ECOLI residues 14–90 and Y143_HAEIN residues 5–81 (Pfam HTH_6, PF01418). 16 of 77 aligned residues are identical (grey ticks) and 56 of 77 have the same class (purple ticks). Its longest same-class run (aligned positions in a row, no gap, same class in both; black box) is 12 residues, the median of all 37,085 pairs at 20–30% identity, which is why it was chosen.
**b**, Shared words on one pair: LPOA_HAEIN (575 aa) and LPOA_VIBCH (603 aa), Pfam LppC (PF04348), 25% identity over the Pfam alignment. Dot plots of the full Swiss-Prot sequences: each dot is a word both proteins contain, placed at its start in each. Left, amino-acid 5-mers (2 shared); right, H/P 19-mers (7 shared). The two word lengths carry about the same information in Swiss-Prot 2026_03 (5 × 4.06 and 19 × 0.97 bits). The black box surrounds a 20-residue same-class run in which 2 residues are identical; 0 shared amino-acid 5-mers lie on its diagonal. The 7 H/P 19-mers overlap on that diagonal and join into one region (purple bar under LPOA_HAEIN): LPOA_HAEIN 280–304 with LPOA_VIBCH 287–311, all 25 positions in the same class, inside the Pfam domain (39–568). The pair was chosen by a rule: of 20 pairs with a run of at least 19 that is not low-complexity and at most 30% identical, the one with the fewest shared H/P 19-mers off that diagonal.
