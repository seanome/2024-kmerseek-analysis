# Abundance comparison added 24 October 2026

Written before inspecting new abundance results. This extends the frozen discovery protocol at Olga’s explicit request to run DE-kupl and gene-level differential expression alongside SPLASH. It does not alter SPLASH’s adjusted-P threshold or anchor population.

Use the same 64 validated trimmed paired-read files and sample IDs as SPLASH. Native DE-kupl supplies unmasked canonical 31-mer counts and Kallisto transcript counts. Derive transcript-to-gene mappings only from the corresponding GTF; stop on an unmatched transcript instead of guessing. Preserve all counts. The 1.3.5 container failed to build before experimental processing; the alternative is the published Transipedia Conda package 1.3.3, with its full environment and source hashes recorded. The known-count check must pass before experimental use. If multiple FASTA records map to one gene, require length-aware aggregation rather than treating their raw summed counts as a validated gene-expression estimate. The blocked external DESeq2 model is distinct from DE-kupl’s native condition-only test, which is not the primary inference.

The primary young contrasts are 2 hours, 24 hours and 13 days (312 hours), separately. At each time, retain strata with exactly one control and one treatment at the same genotype and age. Calendar collection dates are unavailable; genotype/age/time is an operational stratum, not a verified calendar date or a vial-based pairing. Sum the balanced strata within each genotype and condition before fitting `~ genotype + condition`. This produces four, four and three genotype units respectively. Repeated collections do not count as additional independent genotypes. Other times and the old single genotype retain measured counts but have no primary treatment test under this design. Do not combine the published 13/49-day or 41/93-day groups. Stage differences remain visible and limit treatment interpretation; stage measured after treatment is not automatically treated as a baseline covariate.

For each assay and contrast, require at least five pooled counts in at least two genotype/condition units. Use global normalization factors computed over the full eligible pooled matrix. K-mer model fitting may use deterministic hash partitions to bound memory, following DE-kupl’s chunked approach; retain raw p-values from every partition and apply BH once over the whole eligible testing population. No BH correction within a chunk or only within SPLASH hits. Report failed fits and missing p-values explicitly. The decision threshold is adjusted P strictly below 0.05; show log2 fold changes without selecting a new magnitude cutoff from results.

Gene DE and sequence-composition evidence can coexist. An anchor overlapping a DE gene is not proof that SPLASH detected its total abundance change. Candidate splicing requires alignment to an annotated splice junction or alternative transcript structure, followed by target/read evidence. Mismatch or indel alignments are sequence-change candidates, not validated variants. Nonunique mappings, unmatched gene models and insufficient read support remain unresolved. A nonsignificant gene test is reported as no DE detected, never unchanged.

The prepared evidence join maps every emitted extension to the supplied chromosome reference with minimap2, then intersects the matching GTF exons. Before running these alignments, fix MAPQ at least 20, aligned-query coverage at least 0.9, identity at least 0.95 and total nonduplicated exon overlap at least 31 nt. Multiple compatible genes remain ambiguous. An aligned intron matching annotated exon boundaries is a splice-junction candidate, not a differential-splicing call. Definitive differential junction usage and read-level variant validation remain not measured. Gene-DE overlap is reported per contrast beside these sequence labels; no mutually exclusive DE-versus-splicing partition is claimed.
